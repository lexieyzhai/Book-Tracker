# How To Use

Open the GitHub Pages link in Safari, Chrome, or Samsung Internet.

No sign-in is needed to use the app.

The app saves on each device:

- Your iPhone keeps its own saved books and ratings.
- A Samsung phone keeps its own saved books and ratings.
- Your Mac keeps its own saved books and ratings.

If a book cover is found, the app uses Open Library's free cover data. If no cover is found, it shows a drawn fallback cover.
